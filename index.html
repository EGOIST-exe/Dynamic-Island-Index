2.6.8 · Notes stay on top
  • Visible sticky notes reassert their topmost status every 400 ms, like the Island.
  • Notes return to the foreground when other windows cover them, without taking keyboard focus.
2.6.7 · Multi-monitor notes
  • Drag detached notes onto any connected monitor.
  • Always on keeps each note visible on its own screen.
  • Monitor identity, position and matching display scaling are preserved per note.
  • An unplugged monitor gets a temporary fallback; reconnecting restores its notes.
Sticky notes
  • New sticky notes hang from the screen edge, independently of the Island.
  • Move into the dedicated notes area and the notes drop down from the edge.
  • Notes overlap like a real stack. Browse them with the mouse wheel.
  • Each new note automatically gets a different color.
  • Each note has a title, with its creation date beside it.
  • Drag notes anywhere on the screen, then back onto the notes area to hang them at the edge again.
  • A lock on each note keeps detached notes visible.
Longer notes
  • When text reaches the bottom, an extra sheet is taped on automatically, without scrolling.
  • Set the maximum sheets per note (default: 2). Full notes show “Note full”.
  • Notes behind the front note fold their extra sheets away and unfold when brought forward.
Notes toolbar
  • A slim dark-gray notes toolbar drops down with your notes.
  • Icon controls for new note, highlighter, pen, eraser and color selection.
  • The highlighter marks text in your chosen color; the pen draws directly on the note.
  • The eraser removes drawings and highlights.
  • The toolbar lock keeps all notes open.
  • Active tools and locks clearly show their on/off state.
  • Customize and position the notes area and toolbar with a live preview.
  • Adjust the show and hide delays.
  • Notes tuck away when the pointer leaves, just like the Island, and save automatically.
Custom shortcuts
  • Assign shortcuts under Behavior → Keyboard shortcuts: click a field and press your combination.
  • Shortcuts for Island toggle, sticky notes, screenshot, recording start/stop, microphone mute and streamer mode.
  • New shortcuts start unassigned. Set only the ones you need.
  • Duplicate bindings are prevented and conflicts with other apps are reported.
  • The Island briefly confirms actions, such as “Screenshot saved” or “Muted”.
Screen recording
  • Record system audio (music, Discord voices, games) and microphone audio, mixed into one track.
  • Choose input and output devices, including your headphones.
  • Record at up to 60 FPS, instead of 30.
  • Choose Original, 2160p, 1440p, 1080p, 720p or 480p resolution.
  • Bitrate adapts automatically to the resolution and frame rate.

THE LITTLE DETAILS

Good to know.

Why does the installer ask about a certificate?

This release uses a development signing certificate, rather than a publicly trusted publisher certificate. The setup explains this and asks for your consent before adding its public certificate to Windows Trusted People, with administrator approval. Review the prompt before installing.

Does it only work with Spotify?

No. Media controls use Windows media sessions, so other players and browser media can work too when they expose those controls. Online synced lyrics are optional and use LRCLIB.

Can it display every Discord message and call?

Not every one. The optional Discord connection can receive supported client events and learn reported private chats. Complete DM discovery and reliable private-call ringing detection are not available through the current connection. Accept/reject controls use Discord keyboard shortcuts.

Can it start quietly with Windows?

Yes. Autostart runs the app in the background. Open Settings from its tray icon whenever you need them. You can change startup behavior in the app.
